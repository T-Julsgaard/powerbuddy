# PowerBuddy design decisions

The supplied brief establishes the visual world. Mode: Operate. Implement directly in code as requested by its three-pass development workflow; no aesthetic approval round is required.

Porcelain #F6F5F0, white surface, ink #172720, muted #56635C, forest #215B43, water #376C91, divider #DDE2D9. Amber and red are reserved for cautions and errors. One tuned native sans stack with tabular numerals: no available licensed Manrope asset is assumed. Lucide outline icons share a 1.75 stroke. The original mark connects a roofline to an electrical pulse, without a leaf.

Home is composed around a complete monthly estimate, paired utility figures, one next action and a progress glimpse. Open rows and dividers carry data; soft green cards carry actions. Usage uses labelled vertical bar charts with an inspection range control and text alternatives. Planning separates hourly price from monthly consumption. Reward details, input reviews and settings share a restrained bottom-sheet language.

Desktop presents a 393 × 852 screen with an internally scrolling body and fixed tabs; short windows reduce screen height. Mobile removes the frame and simulated status bar and provides a compact presenter drawer. Lock and system home share a quiet abstract coastal wallpaper, large clock, restrained notification surfaces and visible unlock controls.

Motion: 120 ms press feedback, 220 ms navigation, 320 ms sheets and notification arrivals. Unlock is the focal transition. No loops. System preference and Settings reduce spatial movement.

Reference review: Tibber's official app page describes inspectable consumption at month/day/hour resolutions and contextual decisions; this inspired explicit units and inspection, without importing smart-meter features. OpenAI's supplied frontend pages reinforce concrete constraints and render-based verification. Tibber's screenshot URL could not be fetched, so no screenshot-derived claims are made.
