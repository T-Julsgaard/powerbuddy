# PowerBuddy
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the supplied brief: plain JavaScript, CSS and HTML, with a small Node build script that embeds source files and a bundled subset of Lucide icons. No runtime dependency in the deliverable.

## Users and purpose
Danish solo residents and families who need to understand electricity and water use and choose achievable habits. GreenBuddies' software engineering simulation must answer how the household is doing, what it can change and the estimated difference.

## Operating context
Offline executable prototype. Desktop phone simulation and mobile viewport. English copy, DKK, metric units, 24-hour clock, Europe/Copenhagen. Fixed starting date 6 October 2026. Fictional household and synthetic histories.

## Capabilities and constraints
Manual period readings, fictional sample bill, household settings, explainable guidance, feasible laundry plans, self-reported and reading-assessed challenges, points ledger, fictional repair/reuse rewards, persistent local state and validated portable backups. A shared controllable clock powers foreground, home, lock and screen-off notifications. No utility connections, OCR, authentication, cloud, real push messages or hosting requirement.

## Principles
Both resources matter. Distinguish measurements, models and estimates. Recognition should encourage lasting habits without guilt or shopping incentives. Price shifting changes costs, not energy or proven emissions. Preserve missing readings as missing. Changes and claims must be idempotent.

## Brand and inclusion
The brief pins warm Scandinavian utility: calm porcelain, forest green, muted water blue, restrained geometric home/pulse mark and a readable sans family. Four main tabs: Home, Usage, Plan, Rewards. Comfortable touch controls, visible focus, keyboard alternatives and reduced motion.

## Evidence
The user's attached PowerBuddy brief is the authority. There is no existing implementation, licensed Manrope asset or project context file. Example rates are fixtures, not researched Danish tariffs.
